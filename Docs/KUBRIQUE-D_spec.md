# KUBRIQUE-D – Open-Source Hypersonic First-Stage Booster
**Author**: Yakub Abdur-Raheem Ahmed, Birmingham B11 3NL, England  
**Date**: 08 Dec 2025 – public domain (CC0 1.0)

## 1. Concept in one sentence
A **35 m tall, 7 m diameter reusable first stage** that uses **two co-axial rotating-detonation air-breathing collars** plus a **central CH₄/LOX rocket** to reach **Mach 9 at 80 km**, **stage at 3.0 km/s**, and **return to land** – **beating Falcon 9’s staging speed by 30 %**.

## 3. What stays “KUBRIQUE”
- **Tall cylindrical “can” silhouette**  
- **Annular rotating-detonation collars** (the “ring of fire”)  
- **Tesla-disk centrifugal pump + 3-stage axial booster** inside each collar  
- **Rim-generator power** (no batteries)  
- **One-burn, one-can, full reusability**

## 4. What’s new vs. Falcon 9
- **30 % faster staging** → **smaller upper stage**  
- **30 % less propellant** (air-breathing) → **lower $/kg**  
- **Integrated MW-class power** → **no separate turbopump battery**
