# KUBRIQUE Architecture (Conceptual Layout)

KUBRIQUE is built around a cylindrical “can” structure containing a core rocket 
channel and an annular collar region. The goal of this document is to outline 
the layout and relationships between conceptual components so that artists, 
analysts, and worldbuilders can understand the architecture at a glance.

This file contains **no manufacturing details** and is intended for safe,
educational use only.

---

## 1. High-Level Structure

KUBRIQUE vehicles use a vertically stacked arrangement:

1. Nose Section (avionics + RCS)
2. Payload Bay
3. Propellant Tank Block (LOX / CH4 volumes)
4. Intertank Load Ring
5. Central Rocket Channel Volume
6. Annular Collar Region
7. Combined Exhaust Section

This unified cylindrical structure creates the signature “KUBRIQUE Can”.

---

## 2. Conceptual Internal Volumes

The internal architecture can be represented as the following simplified volumes:

### A. Air Inlet Band (Conceptual)
A circumferential band representing airflow entry into the collar.
No ducting geometry is included.

### B. Axial Booster Volume
A cylindrical zone between inlet and collar region. Represents the idea of 
pressure rise without showing compressors, blades, or manufacturable geometry.

### C. Annular Energy Ring (RDE Concept Volume)
A ring-shaped space around the central rocket channel. Represents rotating
energy transfer and detonation-cycle behavior in a conceptual sense.

### D. Rim Generator Volume
A peripheral ring occupying the outer radius of the collar. Represents the idea 
of mechanical-to-electrical energy transfer in simplified form.

### E. Central Rocket Core Volume
A cylindrical channel for general rocket operation. No injector patterns, 
chamber dimensions, or pump geometries are included.

### F. Propellant Volumes
Two stacked tanks conceptually representing:
- LOX (upper)
- CH4 (lower)

No slosh baffles, wall thicknesses, or material dimensions are included.

## 3. Load-Bearing Concept

KUBRIQUE uses:
- cylindrical shell structures,
- ring frames,
- internal bulkheads,
in a conceptual, illustrative manner. No structural calculations or wall 
thicknesses are provided.

## 4. Purpose of This Architecture File

This document exists to:
• Clarify the overall arrangement  
• Support blueprint illustration  
• Help researchers visualize relationships between components  
• Provide a consistent reference for artistic renderings  
• Maintain safety by avoiding actionable geometry  

KUBRIQUE’s architecture is meant to be recognisable, modular, and scalable.
